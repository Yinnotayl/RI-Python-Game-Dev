Info

Controls

  • Move pointer - Rotate and aim the central cube.
  • Left click / Shield - Absorb incoming cubes and prevent damage. Shielding slowly costs score.
  • Right click / Attack - Project the red face outward. Keep enemies inside the field until they overheat.
  • Space / Click - Respawn after game over.

Gameplay

  • Red field - Its heating speed increases as the red face turns closer to edge-on.
  • Enemy impact - An unshielded collision removes one health point and causes an impact burst.
  • Score - Destroy enemies to earn points. The spawn rate rises as your score grows.
  • Health - Reach zero health and the cube shatters. Respawning begins a fresh run.
